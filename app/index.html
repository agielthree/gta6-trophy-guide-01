    Roadmap to the platinum

    Fifty-five trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. The order below is built from the official descriptions alone — which trophies are story chapters and missions, which are side strands, which are collectibles and counted grinds. Work it stage by stage; the platinum pops when the other 54 are done.

    How long is it?

    There is no honest hour count yet — the game released on 19 November 2026 and nobody has finished a full platinum run in public. Expect the story stages to take a normal campaign's length, and the counted grinds (250 stunts, 42 gold shooting-range medals, 100 WAiNK It locations) to take longer than the story itself. This is an estimate, not a measured time.

    Missable trophies

    Eight trophies are flagged possibly missable. The flag comes only from reading the official description: it describes a one-off moment — a partner beside you, a single airport visit, a choice, a failure-then-success — rather than something you can repeat any time. None of these is confirmed missable in the game yet: the game released on 19 November 2026, and no guide has verified them in play. Treat the flag as a warning to do them early, not as a fact.

    Everything else is safe. Story trophies cannot be missed — finish the chapter or mission and they pop. Collectibles, activities and counted grinds can all be done after the story, in free roam.

    About the game & sources

    Verified facts

    • Release: Grand Theft Auto VI released on Thursday 19 November 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version has been announced. Source: Rockstar Games; USA Today launch coverage, 19 Nov 2026.
    • Setting: the state of Leonida — Rockstar's fictional Florida — with Vice City (its Miami) at the centre, plus the Leonida Keys, the Grassrivers wetlands, Mount Kalaga National Park, Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia. Source: Rockstar Games official page.
    • Protagonists: Lucia Caminos, recently released from prison, and Jason Duval, a former soldier who fell in with drug runners in the Leonida Keys — partners in crime after an easy score goes wrong. Lucia is the series' first female lead in a mainline game. Source: Rockstar Games; Associated Press, 17 Nov 2026.
    • The trophy list: 55 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze — as PlayStation publishes them, listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles on 18 November 2026. The two lists agree on every name, description and grade. Source: Exophase; PSNProfiles.
    • Online trophies: none. Every trophy in the official list is earned in single-player; there is no online trophy to chase. Source: the official list itself — no description mentions online play.

    What is an estimate here?

    Every guide in this app is written in our own words from the official trophy description. Where a guide goes beyond the description — a likely place, a likely method, a difficulty rating, a missable flag — it says so, usually with the word estimate. The game is a day old; exact locations, mission layouts and trigger details will be pinned down as players map them, and this guide will be wrong in places. What is not an estimate: the trophy names, descriptions, grades and PSN counters, which come straight from the official list.
